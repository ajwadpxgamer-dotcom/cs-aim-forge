## 🌌 What Is PrecisionPulse?

Imagine a shooting range that fits inside a browser tab, a laboratory where every flick of the wrist becomes a data point, and a coach that never sleeps. PrecisionPulse is an open-source **cognitive reflex and aim training suite** built for esports athletes, casual enthusiasts, rehabilitation researchers, and anyone curious about how fast their brain can talk to their hands.

Unlike conventional trainers that reward raw speed alone, PrecisionPulse measures the *texture* of your reaction: the hesitation before a flick, the drift after a snap, the micro-correction that separates a near-miss from a bullseye. Every session paints a portrait of your neuromuscular signature.

The repository you are reading is the **spiritual successor** to earlier aim-trainer experiments — reimagined from the ground up with accessibility, multilingual reach, and analytical depth that respect the player behind the crosshair.

## 🧭 Philosophy of Practice

Most training tools treat repetition as a blunt instrument. PrecisionPulse treats it as a conversation. The suite asks three questions every session:

1. **Where did your attention rest before the target appeared?**
2. **How did your hand translate intent into motion?**
3. **What changed between your best attempt and your average one?**

Answering these with data — not vibes — is what turns a hobby into a craft. The interface is deliberately quiet, almost monastic, so the only noise in the room is your own improvement curve bending upward.

## 🛠️ Technology Under the Hood

The stack is intentionally lean to keep load times imperceptible:

- **Rendering:** Canvas 2D with offscreen buffering for consistent frame pacing.
- **State:** A tiny reactive store; no heavyweight frameworks burdening the bundle.
- **Analytics:** Pure client-side computation, exported as JSON or CSV.
- **Styling:** Modern CSS with logical properties for right-to-left locale support.
- **Testing:** Visual regression snapshots and timing benchmarks in CI.

Nothing here is precious. If a dependency stops earning its keep, it gets replaced.
